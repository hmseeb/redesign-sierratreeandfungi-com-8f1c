Sierra Tree and Fungi is the independent consulting practice of Christopher Hodge, an ISA Board Certified Master Arborist (WE-14385B), the highest credential the International Society of Arboriculture offers, and a TRAQ qualified tree risk assessor serving El Dorado, Sacramento, Placer, and Amador counties, California.

A consulting arborist diagnoses and documents trees but does no removal work, so the advice is conflict free. I provide tree risk assessments, arborist reports, plant health care, and Oak Technical Reports, and I help property owners make informed decisions, preserve the trees they value, and steward their land.

How consulting works

Scientific Services

Master Level Tree Care

I work with large landowners managing landscape health, property owners evaluating tree failure risk, and tree care professionals who need help diagnosing fungal diseases. Every engagement ends in a written, science based assessment you can act on. The trees I work on are the ones that grow here: valley oak, blue oak, interior live oak, and California black oak through the foothills, and ponderosa pine, gray pine, sugar pine, incense cedar, and Douglas fir above them.

Phase 01, Documentation

Arborist Reports

Permit ready arborist reports written to ISA best management practices: what a county reviewer, an HOA, an insurer, or a buyer needs in order to rely on the finding.

Learn more about Arborist Reports

Phase 02, Risk

Tree Risk Assessments

TRAQ qualified tree risk assessments with a documented rating, for a tree near people or structures where somebody needs a defensible answer rather than an impression.

Learn more about Tree Risk Assessments

Phase 03, Diagnosis

Tree Health Assessments

Diagnosis of a declining tree from the roots up: what is wrong, why, and what to do about it, with a written finding and a plant health care plan you can act on.

Learn more about Tree Health Assessments

Phase 04, Permits

Oak Technical Reports

Need an Oak Technical Report for an El Dorado County building permit that affects native oaks? I prepare the permit ready report the county requires, and I know what its reviewers look for.

Learn more about Oak Technical Reports

Phase 05, Laboratory

Fungal Pathology Reports

Identification and assessment of wood decay fungi and pathogens affecting tree structure and health. Lab informed fungal pathology reports document the organism, the risk it poses, and management options.

Learn more about Fungal Pathology Reports

Phase 06, Stewardship

Plant Health Care

Ongoing care after the diagnosis: fixing the water, soil, and root collar problems behind most decline first, and treating only where a treatment fits what I found. No removal crews, and no work sold that the tree does not need.

Learn more about Plant Health Care

The Underground Network

“Fungi are the drivers of so many positive and negative outcomes in tree health and viability.”Christopher Hodge, ISA Board Certified Master Arborist

Why “And Fungi”?

Why have fungi in my business name? Fungi are the drivers of so many positive and negative outcomes in tree health and viability. I have spent over fifteen years exploring forests studying fungal diversity and working as a consultant for an agricultural amendments company on mycorrhizal species. Being a consultant arborist who has spent almost all my life in El Dorado County, California, I have been able to monitor individual trees for decades and watch how they respond to different wood decay fungi, weather patterns, shifts in land use, and fire. I have spent countless hours searching for spores with a microscope, reading research papers, and talking to a wide variety of experts who have spent their lives in the forest to grasp how we can keep our trees, forests, and ecosystems healthy.
